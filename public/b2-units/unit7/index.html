Exercise B – Choose the correct answer
Choose the correct article A–D. Choose D if no article is needed.
  • 1. Oh, I didn't tell you! We've got ………… new English teacher.
    ✓ A a (indefinite – first mention)
  • 2. Here's ………… DVD you asked to borrow.
    ✓ C the (specific – the one you asked for)
  • 3. We're out of ………… coffee, so could you get some from the supermarket?
    ✓ D no word (coffee = uncountable, general)
  • 4. The prize is ………… unique opportunity to travel the world!
    ✓ B an (book answer: an unique opportunity — some styles accept this)
  • 5. It looks like ………… glass in your bedroom window is cracked.
    ✓ C the (specific glass – in your bedroom window)
  • 6. It's ………… honour to be here this evening to speak to you.
    ✓ B an (an honour – 'h' is silent, vowel sound)
  • 7. There's ………… good chance we'll be late for the meeting.
    ✓ A a (a good chance – indefinite)
  • 8. Reports are coming in of a major oil spill in ………… Mediterranean.
    ✓ C the (seas/oceans always use the)
  • 9. I went to see the doctor because I'm finding it difficult to sleep at ………… night.
    ✓ D no word (at night – fixed expression)
  • 10. Do you think that they'll ever send a manned mission to ………… Venus?
    ✓ D no word (planets – no article)

Exercise H – Choose the correct answer
Choose few, a few, little or a little for each sentence.
  • 1. If you have ………… time at the end of the exam, check your answers.
    ✓ D a little (time = uncountable; positive — some time)
  • 2. Give me ………… minutes and I'll be ready.
    ✓ B a few (minutes = countable; positive — some)
  • 3. We've only got ………… milk left so get some when you go shopping.
    ✓ D a little (milk = uncountable; some remaining)
  • 4. Just ………… practice every day and you'll soon be able to play the piano.
    ✓ D a little (practice = uncountable; positive)
  • 5. We were disappointed that ………… of the members came to the youth club party.
    ✓ A few (members = countable; negative — disappointed = not many)
  • 6. Sprinkle ………… sugar on the strawberries.
    ✓ D a little (sugar = uncountable; positive — some)
  • 7. I suppose now I'm 43, I have ………… hope of playing football for England.
    ✓ C little (hope = uncountable; negative — hardly any)
  • 8. Could you help me with ………… exercises I don't understand?
    ✓ B a few (exercises = countable; positive — some)
  • 9. There's ………… coffee left, if anyone wants some.
    ✓ D a little (coffee = uncountable; positive)
  • 10. Becoming an astronaut is so demanding that ………… people manage it.
    ✓ A few (people = countable; negative — not many manage it)
  • 11. I'll meet you in half an hour – I've got ………… e-mails to write first.
    ✓ B a few (e-mails = countable; positive — some)
  • 12. Many people feel there's ………… point in voting, even in a general election.
    ✓ C little (point = uncountable; negative — hardly any point)

Exercise J – Rewrite the sentences correctly, adding articles where necessary
Rewrite the whole sentence with correct articles. Multiple answers may be acceptable.
  • 1.
    We went to theatre last night and saw great play, although some of acting was bit poor.
    ✓ We went to the theatre last night and saw a great play, although some of the acting was a bit poor.
  • 2.
    Give me ring tomorrow before I go to work and we'll arrange to meet at office.
    ✓ Give me a ring tomorrow before I go to work and we'll arrange to meet at the office.
  • 3.
    Prime minister told reporter that government wanted to pass law banning hunting.
    ✓ The Prime Minister told a reporter that the government wanted to pass a law banning hunting.
  • 4.
    I asked hotel manager where pool was and she directed me to seventh floor.
    ✓ I asked the hotel manager where the pool was and she directed me to the seventh floor.
  • 5.
    They said on news on radio that Mount Vesuvius, volcano that destroyed Pompeii, could erupt again.
    ✓ They said on the news on the radio that Mount Vesuvius, the volcano that destroyed Pompeii, could erupt again.
  • 6.
    Car has revolutionised transport and is absolutely vital to economy of most countries.
    ✓ The car has revolutionised transport and is absolutely vital to the economy of most countries.
  • 7.
    Judge read jury's verdict aloud and then sentenced accused to five years in prison.
    ✓ The judge read the jury's verdict aloud and then sentenced the accused to five years in prison.
  • 8.
    Job in media can be quite stressful because of pressure you are under to do things on time.
    ✓ A job in the media can be quite stressful because of the pressure you are under to do things on time.
  • 9.
    Very few people in 19th century went to school or university and most started work at very young age.
    ✓ Very few people in the 19th century went to school or university and most started work at a very young age.
  • 10.
    I have little free time since I gave up karate, so I'm thinking of trying new hobby, like learning musical instrument.
    ✓ I have little free time since I gave up karate, so I'm thinking of trying a new hobby, like learning a musical instrument.
