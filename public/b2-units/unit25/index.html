Exercise A – Complete using the correct form of the verb in brackets
Use the correct verb form for unreal past, wishes, it's time, I'd rather, suppose, what if, if only.
  • 1. Don't you think it's time you (tell) Andrea the truth?
    ✓ told (it's time + past simple)
  • 2. What if we (rent) a villa by the sea for the summer?
    ✓ rented (what if + past simple = unreal suggestion)
  • 3. I'd rather people (send) me e-mails than (phone) me.
    ✓ sent / phoned (I'd rather + past simple for preferences about others)
  • 4. Suppose you (be going to) open a clothes shop. What kind of clothes would you stock?
    ✓ were going to (suppose + past continuous/unreal)
  • 5. It's time to (go) now.
    ✓ go (it's time + to-infinitive = simple instruction)
  • 6. If you (have) a time machine, which era would you want to go back to?
    ✓ had (second conditional: if + past simple)
  • 7. Shelia would come with us if she (not have to) work tomorrow.
    ✓ didn't have to (second conditional: if + past simple)
  • 8. We'd rather you (not lend) Brenda any more money – she's got to learn not to spend what she doesn't have.
    ✓ didn't lend (I'd rather + past simple for preference about others)
  • 9. It's high time I (leave).
    ✓ left (it's high time + past simple)
  • 10. What if we (get) married? Would that make you happy?
    ✓ got (what if + past simple = hypothetical)
